Four reviewers walked every screen of the redesigned app in the dev mock (`?mock`, simulated Steam Controller, 0.7.137 tree), each slice three times: pad only, keyboard only (`?mock&nopad`), mouse only, at 1440×900 and 1100×700, dark and light theme, two accents. ~1,200 recorded pad steps, ~700 screenshots. ## Verdict

The design is right and most of it works: one grammar (LB/RB tabs, LT/RT sections, A/X/Y per row, B back, View home, Menu tap-save / hold-options), accurate footer hints on the main pages, first item focused on the main pages, pickers and sub-pages that unwind cleanly, number rows that work with all three inputs, light theme and both accents applied everywhere, the on-screen keyboard, Steam import, the wizard's first two steps and the Controller-action picker are all good. The card copy on the four input fronts is the best in the app.

It is not yet "a joy" because of a short list of structural faults that a first-time user hits in the first minutes, and a long tail of copy that still explains controls instead of naming things. The structural list is small enough to fix in a day or two:

1. **Test mode has no way out for mouse or keyboard users, and the pad's "B to stop" is false.** Three different exit instructions, one of them wrong, the right one covered by toasts.
2. **Focus is dropped after actions** (to `<body>`) in a dozen places: after saving, deleting, toggling, importing, closing a picker, and when the Chords list/editor opens — the next D-pad press lands on whatever is behind the open dialog.
3. **Chords, Step 1, captures LT/RT as the button to hold while also advertising LT/RT as "Step".** A chord started as LB ended up saved as RT.
4. **The title bar's status chip and "…" are unreachable by D-pad** from the tab strip; **B / Esc on Home jumps forward into Layout**; **cold keyboard start has nothing focused** and Enter does nothing.
5. **Every edit silently becomes "only for this controller"** while the quick menu says that mode is off. (Known: edits fold into the connected controller's variant by design, but the UI now contradicts itself.)
6. **Fine-tune pages**: Down past the last setting jumps into the group rail and switches group; LT/RT then step while focus stays elsewhere. Stepping Buttons sections with LT/RT scrolls without moving focus. The full-page sub-pages (pickers, Fine-tune, Chords, Key combo, Light pages) render no hint capsule and compensate with "A changes one; Y names or removes it" body copy.
7. **Legacy screens** still in the old family: Sticks ▸ "Picking from a wheel" sheet, Trackpads ▸ On-screen menu ▸ Arrange, Library ▸ More ▸ Launch with game dialog, Hold to swap ▸ Change buttons dialog, Trigger Calibrate (a dead end on a Steam Controller), Menus "Details" wall.
8. **Naming leftovers**: "modes" in the game switcher, Hold to swap explainer, Guides, the Layout footer ("LT RT Mode"), "Choose mode" hints, "Remove this change"; raw JSM ids (MUP/MRING/T Left/LLEFT, STICK_DEADZONE_*, TRIGGER_SKIP_DELAY, "0 / 32767", "Config syntax: G") in pickers, captions and rows; two versions on the Startup/About pages.

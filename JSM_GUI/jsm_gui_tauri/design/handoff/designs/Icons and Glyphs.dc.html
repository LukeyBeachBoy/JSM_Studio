One drawing grammar for every icon and glyph
55 UI icons, 35 mode and category icons, 41 Steam Controller glyphs and three other families, all redrawn from scratch. Files: icons/ui/*.svg, icons/modes/*.svg, glyphs/<family>/<JSM id>.svg.
10aGrammar
Grid24 × 24, 2px live-area padding (red). Keylines: circle Ø20 (green), square 18 r4.5 (purple), portrait 16×19 and landscape 19×16 (amber).
Stroke2px at 24. Optical per size, not scaled: 1.5px at 16, 1.75 at 20, 2 at 24, 2.5 at 36, 3 at 48. Round caps and joins everywhere.
CornersRects r2.5 for containers, r4.5 for pad-like squares, r1 for small inner marks. No sharp corners except the D-pad cross.
FillUI icons are line icons; fill is reserved for state (override dot, unsaved dot, active layer). Controller glyphs are solid badges with knocked-out labels in --glyph-ink.
ConventionSolid = front of controller. Outline = back (paddles, grips) or passive (touch, region). A filled wedge or arm = direction or segment.
Pixel-snapHorizontal and vertical strokes sit on .5 coordinates at 2px so they render on whole pixels at 24 and 48.
10bSize ramp
UI icons keep one drawing with stroke re-tuned per size. Controller glyphs switch to a small cut at 20px and below: single-character labels set larger, inner details dropped, heavier outlines. Small cuts live in glyphs/steam/small/.

10eSteam Controller glyphs
Solid badges for front inputs, outlines for back and passive inputs. Captions are JSM ids used as file and SVG ids. Grips are slanted tabs with grip ridges (L/R by the slant and label); gyro is a gyroscope: upright gimbal ring, horizontal rotor, spin axis on a pedestal. Unfamiliar inputs (grips, paddles, regions) always sit next to their name in the UI.

TURN 11 · APP ICON
JSM Studio app icon
A homage to the JoyShockMapper mark: the split controller, the four dots, the cyan disc and the orbit that stands for gyro, redrawn with one accent, one ink and a proper tile. The orbit weaves behind the left half and in front of the right, and ends in a small satellite. The 16px cut drops the dots, stick ring and satellite so it stays a readable disc-with-orbit in the taskbar and title bar.

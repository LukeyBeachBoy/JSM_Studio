TURN 2 · BUILT ON 1c
The title bar is a focus scope
Everything in the title bar is reachable and operable with the pad alone: Editing, Layer, Applied, Mapping, Undo, Redo, Save and Apply. Menus open under their trigger, take focus on the current value, and hand focus back when they close.

EnterFrom the page tabs, Up moves into the title bar at the item closest horizontally. From anywhere in the page, LT/RT still change page; Up repeatedly always ends in the title bar.
MoveLeft/Right walk 1 → 8 in order. Down returns to the remembered page tab, then the remembered row. The brand mark and window controls are mouse-only.
ActivateA opens a dropdown or runs the button. X on the mapping plate flips mapping on/off without opening it. X on Editing jumps to the applied configuration (through the unsaved-changes guard).
BackB closes an open menu and returns focus to its trigger; B again leaves the title bar and restores the last page focus and scroll.
DisabledUndo/Redo with nothing to undo are skipped. Save with no changes stays focusable and says “No unsaved changes” in the capsule. Apply stays focusable and says “Applied” when the file is already loaded.
ShortcutProposed: View (☰⃞) jumps straight to the title bar from any scope and back again. Needs one extra key in Studio’s pad mapping (open question).

TURN 1 · STRUCTURE
Three shells for JSM Studio
All three share one frameless title bar (context + mapping plate + Save/Apply) and the same Buttons page, so only the navigation model and footer differ. Y on Buttons carries the controller focus. The current app is recreated at reference/Current UI – Buttons for side-by-side.
1aAccordion sidebar · floating capsule
Closest to today's model. Free movement between top-level pages; the active page unfolds its sections in place. Studio pages sit behind a single row at the bottom that swaps the sidebar. Hints float in an inset capsule that never touches content edges.

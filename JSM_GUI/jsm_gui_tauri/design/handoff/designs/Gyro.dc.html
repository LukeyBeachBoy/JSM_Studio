Gyro: the settings-page template
Full-width setting rows: label and origin on the left, value pill right-aligned, a wide slider or segmented control, and the description always underneath. Rows are separated by hairlines only. Minimum sensitivity is shown in adjust mode (A to enter, left/right to change, X for fine/coarse, A or B to leave). Static sensitivity has no description, so the row says so, shows the raw JSM name and offers the docs. Triggers, Joysticks, Trackpads and the Tuning pages reuse this template.

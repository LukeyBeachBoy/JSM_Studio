Overview: the controller is the hero
Callouts are grouped anatomically beside the art (shoulders, grips, paddles, middle buttons), with the sticks, D-pad, face buttons and pads in a band below and quick settings last. Live state draws on the art: right pad touch with trail, right stick, A press pulse, trigger fill in the LT/RT callouts, right grip contact on the back view. The focused callout (RB) lights its hotspot on the art. The hero uses the approved Tonal body rendering (Controller Art 9c), front and back.

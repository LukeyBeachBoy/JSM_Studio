TURN 18 · IN-GAME OVERLAY
Pad and stick menus over games
Built to read in half a second: near-opaque ink regions (rgba(12,16,22,.84)) with a light hairline and shadowed white type work over bright and dark scenes; the region about to fire flips to solid accent with dark type; the live touch dot is telemetry green with a two-step trail. Each region shows label, key and icon (icon slot shown as a square; real icons come from lucide / game-icons). Size and position come from Menu layout. Motion: menu fades and scales 0.96→1 over 160ms on reveal; the hot region cross-fades in 90ms; nothing else moves. Self-contained CSS for the ~6 kB overlay bundle.
